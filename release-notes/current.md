Version: 2.2.0

Vell has a new look: one header with your sections in a navigation island, and a Dictation page that puts today's words beside your history. Push to talk is also fixed for the cases where it kept recording after you let go of the key.

## What's new

- Vell is fast, private, on-device voice dictation. Everything runs on this PC: there is no account, no cloud transcription and no usage limits. Your audio stays on this PC and dictation works offline once your model is downloaded.
- The sidebar and title bar are now one header. Dictation, Dictionary, Writing style and Insights sit in a navigation island in the middle. Settings, Help and notifications are at the right. As the window narrows, the island shortens its labels instead of overlapping anything.
- The Dictation page is now a feed of your dictations, grouped by day, beside a panel with today's words and the typing time they saved, your week and your voice profile. Search is always open and highlights what it finds. Your speaking pace, and the time saved over 7 days, 30 days or all time, are in Insights.
- Push to talk now stops when you let go of the key in two cases where it kept recording: when your PC was busy as you pressed the key, so a long hold looked like a quick tap, and when the key watcher restarted while you held it.
- Scrolling through your dictations is smoother, typing in the search stays quick with a long history, and the Dictation page uses almost no processor while it sits open.

## Installing or updating

**Windows:** Run the installer. Vell is not yet code-signed; if SmartScreen appears, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Mac:** Replace Vell in Applications with the new build. Follow the first-run steps if macOS blocks opening the app.
